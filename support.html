Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. DebtDrop stores all data on your device and your personal iCloud account. We never see your data.

How do I sync across devices?

Enable iCloud on your device in Settings. DebtDrop syncs automatically through your personal iCloud account.

Are the calculations accurate?

DebtDrop uses standard amortization formulas. Results are estimates based on the information you provide. Always verify with your lender for exact figures.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device. You can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of your billing period.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

No Data Collection

DebtDrop does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or any third-party services that collect data.

Local Storage

All data you enter into DebtDrop is stored exclusively on your device. We have no access to your financial information, debt details, income, or any other data you enter.

iCloud Sync

If you choose to enable iCloud on your device, DebtDrop will sync your data to your personal iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. This data is stored in your private iCloud container and is not accessible to us or any third party.

No Account Required

DebtDrop does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app.

No Third-Party Services

DebtDrop does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting tools, or data collection services of any kind.

No Advertising

DebtDrop does not display advertisements and does not share data with advertising networks.

Financial Disclaimer

DebtDrop provides estimates and projections only. Results are based on the information you provide and may not reflect actual outcomes. DebtDrop is not a substitute for professional financial advice.
